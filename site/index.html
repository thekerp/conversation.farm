We farm
conversations.
Not content.

An hour of two people actually thinking, planted, tended, and harvested into something you'd want to send to a friend.

A content farm grows cheap stuff, fast, for an algorithm. We grow one thing at a time, slowly, for a person… who then usually has a thought of their own about it. That's the whole crop.

How it grows

Four beds, one season
Bed 01 — Seed

A question worth an hour

Usually the one somebody won't shut up about. If it fits in a text message, it isn't a seed yet.

Bed 02 — Plot

The conversation

We hit record and talk. No script, no outline… sometimes a guest, if they can hang.

Bed 03 — Harvest

The convo, harvested

The transcript gets a real research pass and comes back as something you scroll: the links, the tangents, the stuff we got wrong.

Bed 04 — Compost

Old talk, back in the dirt

Every conversation stays in the soil. We've got a hundred-odd episodes down there already, and they keep turning up in new beds.
